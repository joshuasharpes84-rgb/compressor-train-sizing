Sizes a multi-stage compressor train (equal pressure ratio per stage, full intercooling back to suction temperature) from Compressor Selection/Sizing methodology — auto-suggests type and efficiency method from your process conditions, then sweeps N = 1-6 stages to recommend the economically optimal stage count (Seider Ch. 16.5 equipment costing + Peters & Timmerhaus payback screen). Change any input and every result recomputes live. See the Compressor Selection, Compressor Train Sizing, and Perry's Compressors presentations for the underlying method.

Known simplifications / gaps (carried from the source workbook)
  • Fig. 10-64 type-selection boundaries are eyeballed from a chart image, not precisely digitized — cross-check borderline operating points against the reference chart.
  • Gas library MW/k are approximate ideal-gas values near ambient — use Custom/Manual Entry for mixtures or non-ideal conditions.
  • Scale-Up method needs external Casey & Robinson friction-factor data not present in this project's source decks.
  • Balje method's chart itself isn't available — ηc must come from an external chart reading.
  • Seider Ch. 16.5 only gives compressor cost equations for Centrifugal, Reciprocating, Screw — every other type shows "n/a - outside Seider correlation coverage," not a fabricated fit.
  • CEPCI defaults to 567 (no escalation) unless you pick a real year.
  • Combined tax rate defaults to 21% flat federal (no state) — editable above.
  • Depreciation life for the after-tax cash flow reuses the evaluation period N, not a separately specified MACRS schedule.
  • The Stage-Count Optimizer holds ηp constant across the N=1-6 sweep — a documented Class-5 (±30-50%) screening simplification, not per-row-accurate.
  • Intercooler sizing uses an assumed overall U and a standard 90/120°F cooling-water basis to back into an area for costing — not a full thermal/mechanical HX design.
