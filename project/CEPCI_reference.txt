Escalation formula (move a cost from one year to another):

    C2 = C1 * (I2 / I1)

    C1 = known cost at year/index basis 1
    I1 = CEPCI value at year/index basis 1 (the year C1 is quoted in)
    I2 = CEPCI value at year/index basis 2 (the year you want the cost expressed in)
    C2 = resulting cost, escalated to year/index basis 2

Notes:
- Only escalate a few years at a time; the index's underlying cost-component weighting
  drifts over long spans, so accuracy degrades the further apart I1 and I2 are.
- Use the annual average for a same-year comparison; use the specific month's value
  (e.g. "Jan. 2002") if that's literally what the source chart/table cites, rather than
  substituting that year's annual average.
- Matches escalate_cost() in scripts/capital_cost_estimation.py, which implements this
  same C2 = C1*(I2/I1) relationship against the CEPCI dict defined at the top of that file.
